# Stack policy

Retain [spawn-agent](../../spawn-agent/SKILL.md) results, launch uncertainty and exact acceptance in the conversation or existing checkpoints. Child work-ticket checkpoints own delivery evidence. TODO retains unanswered questions and next actions. Do not duplicate these as a stack ledger.

Keep the exact ordered ticket identities, initial target/base, canonical criteria and authorized boundary in the conversation and complete successor briefs. Each brief names its predecessor and verified exact base, separate source branch/PR target, finite child limits and evidence requirements. At most one ticket child executes. If conversational authority is unclear after compaction, retrieve the original source or ask rather than reconstructing permission.

Before advancing, inspect original child review/test/CI and release evidence before explicitly accepting the exact revision; no copied check inventories. Incorporate automatic Mailbox reports and preserve unresolved actions in TODO before ACK. Reconcile redeliveries before reapplying effects; no parent mailbox observer or copied counters are needed. Child CI/repair bounds remain at their owning checkpoint.

Historical stack records stay available as evidence. Any explicit takeover first reconciles the former owner, active child, pending effects/questions and any separately owned observer bounds. Never infer fresh authority, delete uncertainty or automatically rewrite/import old records.
