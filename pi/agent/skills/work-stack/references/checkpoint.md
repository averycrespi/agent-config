# Stack policy

[Coordinate](../../../extensions/coordinate/README.md) automatically retains worker identities, launch uncertainty and exact acceptance. Child work-ticket checkpoints own delivery evidence. TODO retains unanswered questions and next actions. Do not duplicate these as a stack ledger or edit Coordinate's private record.

Keep the exact ordered ticket identities, initial target/base, canonical criteria and authorized boundary in the conversation and complete successor briefs. Each brief names its predecessor and verified exact base, separate source branch/PR target, finite child limits and evidence requirements. At most one ticket child executes. If conversational authority is unclear after compaction, retrieve the original source or ask rather than reconstructing permission.

Before advancing, inspect original child review/test/CI and release evidence plus Coordinate's explicit acceptance; no copied check inventories. Inspect the original parent Monitor receipt rather than maintaining wake counters or reservations. An answer or successor does not reset observation authority; child CI/repair bounds remain at their owning checkpoint.

Historical stack records stay available as evidence. Any explicit takeover first reconciles the former owner, active child, pending effects/questions and original observer bounds. Never infer fresh authority, delete uncertainty or automatically rewrite/import old records.
