# Spin Out

Delegate one concrete task to an isolated Pi worker. Before choosing standalone mode, inspect the current conversation and binding/index. In a bound Coordinate coordinator use [`coordinate` spawn](../../extensions/coordinate/README.md) with one self-contained brief and caller-chosen names/path. Preserve existing reporting, observation and evidence-acceptance duties even for one worker; launch mechanics, not a mode switch. Children cannot spawn persistent workers. Enabling Coordinate is a human slash-command decision, never an agent action.

Outside active managed coordination, use the [standalone exact-base launch procedure](references/launch.md) and load [Herdr](../herdr/SKILL.md). Invocation authorizes the local branch/worktree, unfocused workspace, ignored handoff and Pi launch, not publication, cleanup or unrelated work. Standalone questions use ordinary conversation and require no coordinator/index or observer. Retain source availability, ignored handoff readback, identity and task-correlated execution confirmation; submission alone is not execution or completion. Retain resources and reconcile uncertainty, never replay or roll back.

For existing pre-Coordinate managed assignments retain their [legacy recovery procedure](../coordinate-repo/RECOVERY.md) and actual authority; no automatic cutover or adoption. [Work-stack](../work-stack/SKILL.md) adds thin serial predecessor policy. Shared [managed decisions](references/decisions.md) preserve checkpoint-before-report and nonblocking human questions; child-owned CI never replaces parent supervision.
