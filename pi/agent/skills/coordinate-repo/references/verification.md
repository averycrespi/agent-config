## Live validation recipe (requires separate authority)

Unrun unless explicitly authorized and recorded. Obtain scope for three disposable Herdr-managed workers, one coordinator and one explicit coordinator replacement, using candidate source already available. Installation/Stow/reload, real external mutations/publication and cleanup are separate approvals. Set a 15-minute absolute shared deadline, at most six cumulative one-shot attention attempts and no automatic repairs/restarts. Stop at either bound and retain resources/evidence.

1. Record exact candidate head, project/mailbox address, three assignment IDs/revisions, worker/session/process identities, checkpoints, sole coordinator and original allowances. Use shared exact-base launch/readback and mailbox observation before each non-waiting task prompt. Register the mailbox-scoped watcher for A, then persist B/C membership and coverage under the same active observer before their prompts. Verify no membership-only cancellation/re-registration, unchanged allowance and retained registration snapshot. Resolve B/C reports against current project membership, not that snapshot. Do not edit worker checkouts after launch.
2. Give A/B distinct human-only questions and C a short independently verifiable result. Checkpoint-before-report. While the human types an unsent multiline draft, publish both questions and C's result. Verify count/age batching, one coalesced pending attention, unchanged draft bytes and no terminal-input injection or steering. On TUI the draft may defer delivery until submission/settlement; on RPC expect next-human-turn delivery, not immediate automation.
3. Drain the batch after attention: verify exact report identities, persist question references and C's qualified evidence/release disposition, then ack. Leave both questions unanswered. Confirm C can be accepted independently, no modal dialog opens and questions are announced once with distinct handles. Do not infer evidence acceptance from fixture labels or mailbox receipt.
4. Interrupt only at an authorized controlled boundary after project persistence but before ack. Have the outgoing coordinator relinquish control and reconcile its observers. Authorize replacement; recover the same record, pending messages, questions, worker identities, accepted result, exact deadline and consumed/reserved wakes. Verify no duplicate owner, acceptance, announcement or control effect. Reconcile repeated ack safely.
5. Give an ambiguous unlabelled answer, then a uniquely correlated current answer for A. Verify clarification first, real human-message provenance and durable answered-awaiting-relay state. Exercise uncertain relay using retained/synthetic receipt evidence without resending a real prompt. Keep A unresolved until the same worker reports matching application; leave B unanswered through replacement.
6. Publish during an observer registration gap and temporarily omit notifications in an authorized test fixture; initial/polling list must recover retained messages. Inspect a quiet healthy worker versus exited/stale fixture observations using bounded read-only process/checkpoint inspection; no routine model-turn polling, heartbeats, restart or reassignment. Keep child CI observation independent.

Retain actual session traces, editor observations, message/report IDs, project write/ack ordering, control intent/application evidence and original observer receipts. Report failed or unrun seams plainly; do not deliberately manufacture uncertain real writes, bypass approvals or clean up without authority.
