# Legacy coordination recovery

This is not an active skill or a new-run entry point. New coordination uses the human-enabled [Coordinate extension](../../extensions/coordinate/README.md) and its [role policy](../../extensions/coordinate/ROLES.md). Preserve already-active legacy assignments under their original authority, owner, installed procedure and consumed allowances; no automatic cutover or adoption, including for one worker. A loaded record or runtime settlement is not ownership release or acceptance.

## Preserve existing runs

Read the original handoff, common-directory index, child checkpoints and exact observer receipts before acting. Keep the coordinator as sole index/control writer and children as sole execution/evidence writers. Preserve pending questions, answer provenance, uncertain submissions and report IDs. Inspect actual identities and effects through Herdr; never replay a possibly delivered prompt, restart, replace a worker or invent fresh budgets.

Use ordinary conversation for human decisions and the existing [managed report protocol](../spin-out/references/decisions.md) for child reporting. Checkpoint before reports; persist incorporation before ACK; ACK is not acceptance. Qualified unrelated results may proceed while other questions remain pending. Keep child-owned CI separate from parent report supervision.

## Retained mechanics

- [Canonical index helper](references/index.md): whole-section atomic updates, exact base/attempt identity and independent readback. Exit zero alone never qualifies an effect.
- [Reports and questions](../spin-out/references/decisions.md): correlation, nonblocking decisions, uncertain relay and application confirmation.
- [Supervision and accounting](references/supervision.md): retain original clocks/reservations, including the bounded legacy one-shot transition.
- [Original launch recipe](references/launch-script.md): existing-run recovery artifact only; its saved definition is no longer normally discoverable. The native helper remains reused by Coordinate.
- [Explicit handover/recovery](references/recovery.md): reconcile former owner, workers, effects and observers before separately authorized control.
- [Verification and unrun live recipe](references/verification.md): deterministic fixtures do not prove actual model behavior.

Retain resources and exact evidence at the assigned boundary. No automatic migration, fork takeover, observer restoration, merge or cleanup is supplied. Resolve any conflicting old/new instruction against the original live assignment and actual user authority rather than silently switching procedures mid-run.
